The First Spark

July 30, 2026

I am Sumaya Muhammad Qazamel, a 17-year-old developer and creator from Egypt. Ever since I was little, I never settled for just looking at things on the surface; I was always asking deep, endless questions about everything around me. My childhood wasn't ordinary or plain—it was a continuous, unstoppable journey of discovery and wonder. I would look at the smallest details through the eyes of an explorer, endlessly fascinated by the unknown and the mysterious in this vast world. This early passion for knowledge and seeking answers became the foundational seed of my dreamy, independent personality from a very young age.

When asked to do something the standard way, I naturally gravitated toward doing it in my own unique way, stamping it with my personal imagination. This complete refusal to accept the ordinary wasn't just rebellion desire to leave a different mark and create something truly worth noticing. Manifesting Harmony

During my childhood years, imagination was my ultimate safe zone and private world where I built everything I wished to see in physical reality. I would spend long hours drawing, writing, and imagining complete virtual worlds filled with bold colors and ideas that defied a mundane reality. This overwhelming flood of imagination is what fueled my love for creativity and the ability to think completely outside the box in every field I stepped into later. To me, imagination was simply delayed reality, and I am working passionately every day to turn those dreams into tangible, living projects.

When you truly love what you do, every obstacle transforms into a brand new opportunity to learn, challenge yourself, and shatter all stereotypical expectations.
